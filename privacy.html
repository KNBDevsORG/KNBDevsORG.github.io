Privacy policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026.

"Signal Margins Studio" is an internal tool used only by the owner of the Signal Margins YouTube channel to manage that channel's own videos (thumbnails, titles, descriptions and playlists) through the YouTube Data API.

It does not offer accounts to the public, does not collect, sell or share personal data of any third party, and only accesses the YouTube channel of its owner, with the owner's authorization. Authorization tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and can be revoked at any time from the Google account permissions page (myaccount.google.com/permissions).

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. YouTube API Services are subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.
